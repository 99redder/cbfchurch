Monthly Blog: Fear Not, Little Flock

September 21, 2026 CBF

"Fear not, little flock, for it is your Father's good pleasure to give you the kingdom.” (Luke 12:32)


  As the 25th anniversary of September 11, 2001, approaches (being written on Labor Day weekend), we remember the intensity of fear and grief that gripped, not only this country, but around the globe. That trepidation continues to this day in various forms all over this planet as evil continues on its embolden path of terror. The reality of fulfillment of Bible prophecy cannot be denied as we observe “…men’s hearts failing them from fear and the expectation of those things which are coming on the earth…” (Luke 21:26)

  Fear is one of several natural human emotions. However, fear is an extraordinarily complex emotion with a broad spectrum of responses. At one end of the scale, fear can be caused by dread, terror, or intimidation by adversaries. Such fears can lead to debilitating phobias and neuroses. On the other end of the “fear scale” is reverential fear. This fear is motivated by love and is a wholesome concern of displeasing God. Fortunately, most forms of fear are motivational, and therefore, beneficial rather than incapacitating.

 

 It is the fears and anxieties that affect us mentally, physically, and spiritually that Scripture encourages us to overcome by the power of the Holy Spirit. In speaking about the humanity of our Lord Jesus Christ, the writer to the Hebrew Christians says: “Inasmuch then as the children have partaken of flesh and blood, He himself likewise shared in the same, that through death He might destroy him who had the power of death, that is, the devil, and release those who through fear of death were all their lifetime subject to bondage.” (Hebrews 2:14-15)


 Indeed, one of the most prevalent fears is the fear of death. It's a fear which debilitates and imprisons individuals. But recognizing and accepting the fact that Jesus conquered death by his atoning sacrifice on the cross at Calvary, liberates us from that prison of fear. In powerfully triumphant language, Apostle Paul reminds us of this profound ultimate victory - “So when this corruptible has put on incorruption, and this mortal has put on immortality, then shall be brought to pass the saying that is written: ‘Death is swallowed up in victory.’ ‘O Death, where is your sting? O Hades, where is your victory?’ The sting of death is sin, and the strength of sin is the law. But thanks be to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ.” (1 Corinthians 15:54-58)

 

 One of the most profoundly beautiful, compassionate, and reassuring statements Jesus ever made to His disciples is found in Luke 12:32 –“Fear not, little flock; for it is your Father's good pleasure to give you the Kingdom.” Isn't it absolutely amazing that it is our Heavenly Father's intense pleasure, the Greek (Gr. eudoke - means it is God's intense pleasure to give us His “good gift”, the Kingdom!) And so, our loving Savior, as the “great Shepherd” of our souls encourages us not to fear because the Kingdom is ours.

  

So, brethren, let us lift up our heads and rejoice knowing full well that our redemption draws near. These events in the world must and will happen in preparation for the establishment of God's eternal and righteous Kingdom.

  

﻿“See that you do not refuse Him who speaks… from heaven, whose voice then shook the earth; but now He has promised saying, ‘Yet once more I shake not only the earth but also heaven.’ Now this, ‘Yet once more’, indicates the removal of those things which are being shaken, as of things that are made, that the things that cannot be shaken may remain. Therefore, since we are receiving a Kingdom which cannot be shaken, let us have grace, by which we may serve God acceptably with reverence and godly fear.” (Hebrews 12:25-28)

 

 “So, we Your people and sheep of Your pasture will give You thanks forever: we will show forth Your praise to all generations.”  (Psalm 79:13)
