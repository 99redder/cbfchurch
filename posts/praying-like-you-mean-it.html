Praying Like You Mean It

December 8, 2019 DRG
Jesus Christ said, "Ask, and it will be given to you; seek, and you will find; knock, and it will be opened to you" (Matthew 7:7 *NKJV).

Dear friends,
As it has often been said, prayer is one of the most powerful things on Earth!

Therefore I say to you, whatever things you ask when you pray, believe that you receive them, and you will have them. This is because; the eyes of the Lord are on the righteous and His ears are open to their prayers;
( Mark 11:24 ) & ( 1 Peter 3:12 )

Now these verses do not mean that everything we may pray for we will get, however they do mean that if it is God's will for us, our prayer requests will be answered! After all God knows what is best for us, and sometimes what we pray for would end up hurting us in the long run. So trust in God's judgment, after all only He knows the future!

So always; Devote yourselves to prayer, being watchful and thankful. And pray for us, too,
that God may open a door for our message, so that we may proclaim the mystery of Christ. ( Colossians 4:2-4 )
Also please know, We always thank God for all of you, mentioning you in our prayers.
We continually remember before our God and Father your work produced by faith, your labor prompted by love, and your endurance inspired by hope in our Lord Jesus Christ. 
(1 Thessalonians 1:2-3 )

May you have a Wonderful Weekend that is filled with Love, Hope, Faith and Prayers too! Amen.
